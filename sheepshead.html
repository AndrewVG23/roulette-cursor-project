Sheepshead

Five-handed Wisconsin sheepshead. Call an ace, or go alone, and take 61 points. Part of the free Cash 4 Copper browser game world.

Night drive

Deal a hand. Queens and jacks are trump, then diamonds.

How the hand pays

Five players, 32 cards. Trump, high to low: queen of clubs, spades, hearts, diamonds, then the jacks in that same suit order, then the diamond ace, ten, king, nine, eight, seven. In a fail suit the ace beats the ten, and the ten beats the king. Follow suit. Trump is its own suit.

The picker buries two cards, then calls a fail ace or goes alone. The picker and the called ace need 61 points. Under 31 for the losers is a schneider. No tricks is schwarz. The hand is worth 2, plus 1 for each of those. Each loser pays each winner that many points. If everyone passes, the fewest points wins a leaster.
